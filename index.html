Foot care that keeps residents moving

Foot problems are among the most common issues older adults face, and many of them are preventable.

Routine nail and callus care, diabetic foot checks and early wound management improve residents’ comfort and mobility and help reduce fall risk. We also assess fall risk for every resident we see and tell your team about anyone at higher risk.

  • Routine careNail and callus care on a recurring schedule.
  • Diabetic foot careRegular checks for higher-risk residents, with early attention to problems.
  • Wound managementEarly treatment of foot wounds, coordinated with your nursing team.
  • Fall risk assessmentsEvery resident we see is assessed, and higher-risk residents are flagged to your staff.
  • Insurance billingWe bill Medicare and most major plans directly, so your staff has little to no paperwork.

How working with us goes

Getting started takes one conversation. After that, visits run on a schedule your team sets.

  1. Talk with us

    A 15-minute call or short visit to learn about your residents and your team’s needs.

  2. Set a schedule

    We agree on a recurring visit schedule that fits your facility’s routine.

  3. We visit on site

    A podiatrist sees residents at your facility, so no one needs outside transport.

  4. We document and bill

    Notes go to your nursing team and attending physicians, and we bill insurance directly.

Already have a podiatry provider?

We can work alongside them rather than replace them. Many facilities have more qualified residents than a single provider can see on a regular schedule.

We add capacity so every eligible resident is seen on time, and we can cover when your current provider is unavailable.
